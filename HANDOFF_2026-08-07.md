## 5. The real forward work — monitoring, and why you specifically can do it

The owner's words on 08-07: *"i have serious concern that brain is not working in a right way…
set a mechanism where it will keep monitoring and improving brain capabilities and i don't want
to send screenshots of my whatsapp again and again."*

**The finding that shapes this: the monitoring layer already exists and is blind.** Seven jobs
are registered and running in `src/server.ts` — `waIngestHealthAudit`, `waOutboundReconciliation`,
`gapDetectionJob`, `reflectionJob`, `delegationRecoveryJob`, `trustPromotionJob`,
`agentActionReaper`. **Every one of ~35 defects on 08-06/07 still reached us because the owner
reported it. None was raised by a job.** Do not add an eighth. Fix why these are blind:

1. **They watch components, not outcomes.** For Hamna's reply the WA session was healthy, ingest
   was healthy, correlation worked, the thread consumed it — all green, and the owner was never
   told. Nothing measures *the loop*.
2. **They write to a table nobody opens.** Gap detection "persists gap candidates for admin
   review." There is no reviewer.
3. **They cannot see meaning.** A well-formed message with fabricated content passes every
   health check that exists.

### The agreed design, in build order

**(a) Diagnostic truth belongs in a table, not a log.** This is the prerequisite and it is why
nothing was recoverable. The two most important pieces of evidence on 08-06 —
`inbound consume failed, reason: illegal_transition`, and Hamna's 14:00 reply — existed **only in
PM2 logs**: unqueryable, rotated, gone. Every place that currently `log.warn`s a real failure
must also write a `brain_health_finding` row: what failed, which ask, the state at the time, the
evidence. Channel-independent, useful regardless of anything else.

**(b) Make "the ask" the monitored unit.** Not messages, not connectors — the ask, from the
owner's instruction through to *"I told you it's done."* Expected timing at each hop; anything
that stalls is a defect automatically, whatever the components report. This is the owner's
DEF-081 ask (*"keep record what was asked through Brain and when it notified, then it will be
closed"*) — that ledger **is** the monitor, and `owner_notified_at` in `db1e9e7` is its first
column.

What (b) would have caught on 08-06 without the owner: the reply consumed but never passed on;
prompt 276 stuck `queued`; two messages to Hamna in different formats from one ask; her 14:00
reply that produced no row at all. Four of the worst, from one mechanism.

**(c) Invert the reporting direction.** Brain sends the owner a short daily WhatsApp — *"3 asks
stalled, 1 reply I never passed on, 2 messages that broke format."* This is the literal answer to
"no more screenshots": he stops being the detector. No dashboard to remember to open.

**(d) Every defect becomes a scenario, then variations of it.** The owner's deepest complaint,
in his words: *"the case i report you rectify it, but when there is a bit change in scenario brain
got lost."* Patching an instance can never fix that. `tests/brainRegression.test.ts` already
enforces archive-entry ↔ scenario pairing; the missing piece is generating perturbations —
different name, two counterparts, reply arrives before the ask completes, counterpart replies
twice — and running them before he hits them.

## 7. Standing owner rulings — these are invariants, not preferences

Violating any of these has already cost real time. Several are recorded as their own defects.

- **No hardcoded judgement.** Criticality, substance, urgency, "is this ambiguous" — all
  LLM-with-context. A regex is at most a fast pre-filter, never the decision boundary.
- **No hardcoded fake-Brain replies.** Every Brain-surface reply is LLM-generated or a bracketed
  system marker. Hardcoded English sentences pretending to be Brain are forbidden. If you find
  one, audit and remove all sibling instances in the same commit.
- **Brain never speaks as the owner.** No message from his identity without an explicit
  user-initiated chain. Structural guard: the `SendProvenance` enum.
- **No fabrication.** Cite only from the given `dataBlocks`. Empty block → "no data"; absent
  block → "I wasn't given that". Never bridge "contact exists" → "they sent a message".
- **One implementation per rule.** Seven defects so far were one rule with several
  implementations (DEF-039, 041, 044, 045, 051, 074, 078). **Finding two of four is worse than
  finding none, because it looks fixed.** Grep for every call site before declaring a fix.
- **Surface data parity.** Day Brief, Brain Chat and WhatsApp must answer the same question the
  same way; everything routes through `reasoningComposeWithTools`. Surface-specific composer
  shortcuts always surface later as "hallucination" — fix the plumbing, not the prompt.
- **Confirm only when abnormal or risky**, after naming the risk, and gated on confidence
  (DEF-038 → DEF-079, `confirmation.min_confidence_pct`).
- **Outbound template:** `Hi,` / body / blank line / brain name / `<owner>'s Assistant`. Names
  resolved per user, never hardcoded. ONE renderer: `outboundMessageTemplate.ts`.
- **Text, never voice** by default (DEF-084). Voice is opt-in via
  `brain.voice_prompt_min_criticality`.
- **Channel tone separation** — tone samples come from the SAME channel as the destination.
  Never pool WhatsApp samples into an email draft.
- **WhatsApp calling is out of scope** by owner decision. Do not re-open it.
- **Never touch or commit:** `.env`, tokens, `whatsapp-sessions/`,
  `server/docs/nexeo_self_learning&development.md`, `tenant-scope-audit-2026-05-22.md`.
