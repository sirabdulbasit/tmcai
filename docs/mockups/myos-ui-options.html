Information Architecture & Chat Density — direction options

Three variants for each, side by side. My recommendation marked with the orange ring.

1. Information architecture (the left rail)

You have 8-10 tabs that will grow over time (brain, brief, open items, pending approvals, execute action, wiki, custom actions, health + future: settings, agents, logs, reports). Three ways to organise them:

A. Flat list
baseline
One icon per tab, listed top-to-bottom with no grouping. Simplest, but 10+ items all competing equally for attention.

Concrete example — Abdul's morning flow with Option B:
Open Morning Brief (Work) → see 3 priorities → click one → hits Action Center (same group, easy glide) → approve action in Pending Approvals (still in Work). All one eye-movement.

Later in the day → check what the AI learnt → jump to Wiki (Knowledge group, clearly separated). Never mingled with the "act" bucket, so it reads as a context switch, which it is.

Weekly review of system health → Ops group, bottom of rail. Out of the way unless you need it.

Concrete example — same 6-turn conversation in three densities:
• Comfortable fits 2 messages per screen — reads like a letter; good when you want to really think about an answer.
• Compact (recommended) fits 3-4 — matches how people actually use Claude / ChatGPT; enough room to breathe, enough density to keep context visible.
• Dense fits 6+ — faster to re-scan but every message looks like every other message; tires the eye after a few minutes.

Most of your queries will be short ("what's the status of X?") — Compact feels right for those and still reads well on long synthesis answers.
